About

I am an Assistant Computer Scientist in the Mathematics and Computer Science Division at Argonne National Laboratory. I architect high-performance workflow ecosystems where efficiency, transparency, and reliability are intrinsic design properties — and I want Agentic AI to enter science on principled terms, so our computing infrastructures behave like trustworthy, accessible scientific instruments.
